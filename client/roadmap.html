HIGH-VALUE SUGGESTED ADDITIONS

5 PROPOSALS
Ethos-Aligned Enhancements
1. Gamepad & Mobile Touch ARPG Controls SUGGESTION

HTML5 Gamepad twin-stick support (Left Stick = Move, Right Stick = Aim, Bumpers = Potions) plus low-profile virtual thumbstick for phone browsers and Steam Deck.

Why: Instant zero-install play across handhelds and tablets.
2. Dedicated Headless Server CLI Flags SUGGESTION

CLI arguments: node server/server.js --port=8080 --db=/data/saves/sanctum.db --headless --benchmark for clean headless systemd or container management.

Why: Standardizes homelab DevOps and automated smoke tests.
3. Pluggable Community Mod Packs (/mods) SUGGESTION

Subfolder content pack injector (/mods//mod.json) allowing creators to add classes, affixes, monsters, or songs without touching core vanilla JSON.

Why: True adherence to the "Moddable" motto without merge conflicts.
4. Torment Dungeon Map Mutators (Endgame Affixes) SUGGESTION

Procedural floor mutators on Torment NG+ tiers (e.g. Abyssal Darkness: -35% light radius, Volatile: exploding corpses, Frenzied: +30% mob speed/MF).

Why: High endgame replayability with zero asset footprint.
5. Accessibility & Sensory Control Panel SUGGESTION

User toggles in options: screen shake intensity (0-100%), high-contrast silhouette mode, photosensitivity strobe damper, and colorblind palette ramps.

Why: Quality control and player comfort across all screen types.
